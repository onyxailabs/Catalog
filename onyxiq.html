Private by design

OnyxIQ runs entirely in your browser. There is no OnyxIQ server: your channel data travels only between YouTube's official APIs and your own device, and is never sent to us. Signing in is read-only; the single editing feature (bulk metadata edits you type yourself) asks for its own separate permission at the moment you use it, forces a full backup first, and includes one-click undo.

No sign-in warning? Use your own free API key (BYOK)

Setup in 2 minutes, no sign-in warning: OnyxIQ works with your own free YouTube Data API key (BYOK). You paste your key, the tool talks to YouTube from your own Google Cloud project — so you never sign in through our app, never see an 'unverified app' screen, and never count toward any user limit. Your key, your free quota, your data stays in your browser.
