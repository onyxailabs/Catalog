OnyxIQ — Privacy Policy

OnyxIQ — YouTube Growth Studio (Chrome extension) · Last updated: 21 October 2026

The short version: OnyxIQ runs entirely inside your own browser. There is no OnyxIQ server. Your YouTube data is fetched directly from Google's official APIs to your browser, processed locally, and never sent to us or to any third party. We cannot see your channel data — by design, not just by promise.

1. What data the extension accesses

When you choose to sign in with YouTube, OnyxIQ requests read-only access through Google's official OAuth consent screen:

  • youtube.readonly — to view your channel, videos, playlists and comments so the extension can analyse them for you.
  • yt-analytics.readonly — to view your channel's analytics (views, watch time, traffic sources, click-through rate) for the Full Channel Checkup reports.

One additional scope, youtube.force-ssl, is requested only at the exact moment you yourself initiate a bulk metadata edit inside the Bulk Suite, and only after you approve that prompt. It is never requested during sign-in and is never used for anything else.

If you instead use your own YouTube Data API key, or use Demo mode, no sign-in data is accessed at all.

2. Where your data goes

  • All processing happens locally in your browser. OnyxIQ has no server, no database and no analytics of its own.
  • Your data is stored only in your browser's local extension storage (settings, cached reports, rank history, your license key).
  • Your data is never sold, shared, rented or uploaded to Onyx AI Labs or to any third party.
  • The only network requests the extension makes are to Google / YouTube APIs — and, if you choose to add your own Google Gemini API key for the AI Studio, to Google's Gemini API using your key. API keys you enter never leave your browser.

3. YouTube API Services

OnyxIQ uses YouTube API Services. By using OnyxIQ you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. OnyxIQ's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google's own handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Your control

  • You can revoke OnyxIQ's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling the extension deletes all of its locally stored data from your browser.
  • You can clear the extension's stored data at any time from its Settings page.

5. Children

OnyxIQ is a professional tool for YouTube creators and is not directed at children under 13.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new "last updated" date. Material changes will also be noted in the extension's changelog.
